Who She Is

Research, Running, and Accessible Spaces

Samantha Judge, often listed as Sam Judge in event programs, is a New York–based sensory-inclusion advocate whose public work centers on making large venues usable for people with sensory needs. She serves on the Board of Directors of KultureCity, the nonprofit behind sensory-inclusive programs at stadiums, arenas, and concert halls.

Her most visible project is a permanent one: the Madison Square Garden sensory room, which opened in 2024 and carries her name alongside her husband’s. The space gives guests who experience sensory overload a quieter place to regroup during games and concerts without leaving the building.

That advocacy rests on an academic foundation. Samantha completed graduate research in kinesiology at Fresno State examining UCL injury risk in Major League pitchers, work recognized as the university’s Outstanding Master’s Degree Project in 2017. Her full professional biography covers that research, her marathon record, and her roots in Linden, California.
