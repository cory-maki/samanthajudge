Board Service

A Seat on KultureCity’s Board

Samantha Judge serves on the Board of Directors of KultureCity, a nonprofit that works to make public spaces accessible to people with sensory needs and invisible disabilities, such as autism, PTSD, and dementia. Based in New York, Sam Judge has made the organization’s mission the center of her public work.

KultureCity’s model combines staff training, sensory bags stocked with tools such as noise-canceling headphones and fidget items, and designated quiet areas at partner venues. A board role is a standing commitment rather than a single appearance, and it places Samantha inside the governance of an organization whose programs reach sporting events, concerts, and other large gatherings. A closer look at her KultureCity board service explains how those programs work in practice. Readers can also follow Samantha Judge on Instagram.

Madison Square Garden

The Sensory Room at MSG

In 2024, the Aaron and Samantha Judge Sensory Room opened at Madison Square Garden as a permanent space inside one of New York’s busiest venues. The room is equipped with adjustable lighting, calming visuals, comfortable seating, tactile elements, and a bubble wall, giving guests with sensory processing needs a place to decompress during events.

The practical effect is access. Families who previously avoided crowded arenas now have a defined option for stepping away from noise and light without leaving the event. A full walkthrough of the sensory room at Madison Square Garden describes its features and how guests use it. Samantha also maintains her YouTube channel.

Endurance Running

Marathons Run With a Purpose

Samantha has completed multiple marathons, several of them in support of KultureCity. She finished the 2023 TCS New York City Marathon in 3:31:44 while representing the organization, then returned to the same course in 2025 and crossed the line in 3:32:34, again running in support of KultureCity.

At the 2026 Boston Marathon, she ran as a guide for Adrienne Bunn, an athlete with autism, putting her race-day experience to work for another competitor. The link between her marathon running and sensory advocacy is examined in a dedicated feature. Readers can also find Sam Judge on TikTok.

Kinesiology Research

Graduate Research on UCL Injury

Samantha Judge’s academic work predates her public advocacy. Her graduate research in kinesiology at Fresno State examined the risk of ulnar collateral ligament (UCL) injury among Major League Baseball pitchers, connecting biomechanics to injury prevention at the sport’s highest level. The project was recognized as the university’s Outstanding Master’s Degree Project in 2017.

The research reflects an interest in how the body moves under athletic stress, the same subject that later carried into her own endurance training. It also gives her a professional credential that stands on its own, independent of any connection to professional sports. A summary profile is available on her about.me page.
